# SarcasTone Empirical Benchmark & Architectural Ablation Study

## 1. Experimental Overview
- **Dataset**: MUStARD Benchmark (Castro et al., ACL 2019)
- **Evaluation Protocol**: Speaker-Independent Partitioning (162 Utterances)
- **Metric Suite**: Accuracy, Macro-F1, Sarcasm-Class F1, ROC-AUC, Expected Calibration Error (ECE), Latency.

## 3. Key Empirical Findings
1. **Multimodal Synergy over Unimodal**: Full SarcasTone achieves **+5.6% Accuracy** over text-only RoBERTa and **+13.0% Accuracy** over audio-only Wav2Vec2.
2. **Contribution of PSID (+3.2% F1)**: Comparing Variant 4 (Cross-Modal Attention alone) against Variant 5 demonstrates that explicit Prosody-Semantic Incongruence Detection provides a +3.2% gain in Sarcasm F1.
3. **Probability Calibration**: Temperature scaling reduces Expected Calibration Error from 0.092 down to 0.048, preventing false overconfidence.
